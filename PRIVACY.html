EN Privacy Policy

Summary. PixWit is a local-first Windows application for screenshots, scrolling capture, screen recording, image viewing and on-device OCR. It has no account system and no in-app purchases. We do not collect personal data, do not upload your screenshots, recordings, images or OCR text, and do not read device identifiers or hardware fingerprints.

  1. Overview. All core features run on your device. We operate no cloud service that receives your content.
  2. What we do not collect. Account or identity information; screenshots, recordings, images or recognized text; clipboard contents (used locally only, when you paste); device fingerprints or hardware identifiers (machine GUID, MAC address, CPU ID, motherboard serial, hardware UUID); location data; advertising or analytics identifiers.
  3. Local processing and storage. Screenshots, recordings, image editing, pin-to-screen notes, QR scanning and OCR are processed locally. Your settings, history and diagnostic logs are stored on your device (under your user AppData folder) and are never uploaded by us.
  4. Network use. The app performs no cloud AI requests and no telemetry. The only network access is downloading an FFmpeg component (required for screen recording) from our official open-source repository on first use — this is download-only and transmits no personal data. The local OCR models are bundled inside the app.
  5. Third parties. We do not share, sell or transfer your data to any third party, because we do not receive it.
  6. Children. The app is not intended for children under 13.
  7. Your rights. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; you may still contact us with any question.
  8. Changes. Any change to this policy will be posted on this page.
  9. Contact. taoge_ndt@163.com

ZH 隐私政策（中文）

摘要. PixWit 是一款"本地优先"的 Windows 应用，提供截图、滚动长截图、屏幕录制、图片查看与本地文字识别（OCR）。本应用无账号系统、无内购；我们不收集个人数据，不上传你的截图、录屏、图片或识别文本，也不读取设备指纹等硬件标识。

  1. 概述：所有核心功能都在你的设备本地完成，我们没有任何接收你内容的云端服务。
  2. 我们不收集哪些信息：账号/身份信息；截图、录屏、图片或识别文本；剪贴板内容（仅在你粘贴时本地使用）；设备指纹/硬件标识（MachineGuid、MAC 地址、CPU ID、主板序列号、硬件 UUID）；位置信息；广告或统计标识。
  3. 本地处理与存储：截图、录屏、图片编辑、便签贴图、二维码识别、OCR 全部本地处理；设置、历史记录与诊断日志保存在你的设备上（用户 AppData 目录），我们不会上传。
  4. 联网说明：本应用不发起任何云端 AI 请求，也不做遥测。唯一的网络访问是在首次使用录屏功能时，从我们的官方开源仓库下载 FFmpeg 组件（录屏所必需）——仅下载、不传输任何个人数据。本地 OCR 模型已内置在应用中。
  5. 第三方：我们不会与任何第三方共享、出售或转移你的数据（因为我们根本收不到）。
  6. 未成年人：本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。
  7. 你的权利：由于我们不持有你的任何个人数据，因此不存在需要访问、更正或删除的内容；如有疑问可随时联系我们。
  8. 政策更新：本政策如有变更将在本页面公布。
  9. 联系我们：taoge_ndt@163.com
