# English Version

## Privacy Policy

PixWit is **free, has no in-app purchases, and requires no registration or account**. This policy explains how we handle information when you use PixWit.

## 1. Introduction

PixWit follows a data-minimization principle. **All features (screenshot, scrolling capture, screen recording, video editing, image viewing/editing, local OCR, annotation, pin-to-screen notes, QR scanning) are processed locally and offline on your device.** We do not upload your screenshots, screen recordings, images or recognized text to any server.

## 2. Information We Collect

- **Feature data (local, not uploaded).** Screenshots, screen recordings, image viewing/editing, local OCR (PP-OCR offline models), annotations, pin-to-screen notes, and QR scanning are all processed **locally on your device**.
- **Diagnostic logs (optional, local).** To troubleshoot issues, we may record technical information such as app version, operating system type/version, and crash details in local log files (if you enable crash feedback).
- **Payment information.** The app has **no in-app purchases**; we do not collect or store any payment information.
- **On-demand components.** OCR models or FFmpeg may be downloaded from our open-source repositories/GitHub to update local files; this does not involve your personal content.

**We do not require you to register an account, so we do not collect identity information (name, email, etc.), except for information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.**

## 3. Local Processing

Screenshot, scrolling capture, recording, image viewing/editing, local OCR and the other features run **fully offline**. Content remains on your device (the app uses standard system directories for logs and cache). None of this processing transmits data to any server.

## 4. Network Usage

The app needs the network **only** in the following situations, and never for uploading your content:

- When you **actively** use the on-demand component download (FFmpeg / OCR models), the app fetches those component files from the project's open-source repositories (GitHub / Gitee).
- When you open the online help or issue pages from the tray menu, your system browser opens the corresponding web page.

No image, recording, recognized text or other content of yours is transmitted. The app does not integrate any cloud or AI service — there is no cloud OCR, no online translation, no table extraction and no AI analysis.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Sharing

- The app has **no in-app purchases**; we do not integrate payment services and do not collect payment information.
- We do **not** forward your content to any third party, and we do not integrate third-party AI or cloud providers.
- Apart from the above, we **never** sell, share, or use your content for advertising.

## 6. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve features, ensure security and prevent abuse, troubleshoot, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use your content for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

## 7. Storage and Protection

- Because **no registration is required**, we do not store account data.
- All feature data (screenshots, recordings, recognized text) is stored **locally on your device** in the directories you choose or the app's standard directories.
- Logs / diagnostic information are kept for [e.g. 30 days] and then deleted.
- Component downloads use HTTPS encryption and we apply reasonable access controls.

## 8. Your Rights

Because no registration is required, we hold no account data. You may contact us at any time to query or delete any data we may hold that is associated with you. Contact: taoge_ndt@163.com.

## 9. Minors

The app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. If you are a minor, please use it under the guidance of a guardian.

## 10. Contact Us / Policy Updates

- Email: taoge_ndt@163.com
- We will post any changes to this policy on this page; material changes will be notified separately.
- Updated: 2026-09-11

# 中文版

## 一、引言

感谢使用 PixWit。本应用**无内购、无需注册**，并遵循"最小必要"原则。**全部功能（截图、长截图、录屏、视频编辑、看图编辑、本地 OCR 识别、标注、便签贴图、二维码识别等）都在你的设备本地离线完成**，不会上传你的截图、录屏、图片或识别结果。

## 二、我们收集哪些信息

- **功能数据（默认本地、不上传）**：截图、长截图、录屏、视频编辑、图片查看/编辑、本地 OCR（PP-OCR 离线模型）、标注、便签贴图、二维码识别等均在**你的设备本地**完成。
- **诊断日志（可选，保存在本地）**：为排查故障，我们可能把应用版本、操作系统类型/版本、崩溃信息等技术性日志写入本地日志文件（如你选择开启崩溃反馈）。
- **支付信息**：本应用**无内购**，我们不收集、不存储任何支付信息。
- **按需下载的组件**：OCR 模型或 FFmpeg 等组件可能从我们的开源仓库/GitHub 下载，仅用于更新本地文件，不涉及你的个人内容。

**我们不需要你注册账号，因此不收集账号、姓名、邮箱等身份信息（除你主动联系我们时提供的信息）。**

## 三、本地处理说明

截图、长截图、录屏、看图编辑、本地 OCR 识别等核心功能**完全离线运行**，内容只存在你的设备（应用使用系统标准目录保存日志与缓存）。以上处理不向任何服务器传输。

## 四、联网说明

本应用仅在以下情况下需要联网，且**从不上传你的内容**：

- 当你**主动使用**"组件下载"（FFmpeg / OCR 模型）时，应用会从项目的开源仓库（GitHub / Gitee）获取这些组件文件；
- 当你从托盘菜单打开在线使用手册或问题反馈页面时，会由系统浏览器打开对应网页。

你的图片、录屏、识别文本等内容不会被传输。本应用**不接入任何云端或 AI 服务**——没有云端 OCR、没有在线翻译、没有表格提取、没有 AI 解读。

## 五、第三方服务与共享

- 本应用**无内购**，不接入支付服务，也不收集支付信息；
- 我们**不会**把你的内容转发给任何第三方，也不接入第三方 AI 或云服务商；
- 除上述外，我们**不会**把你的内容出售、共享或用于广告。

## 六、信息的使用

用于：提供并改进功能、安全与防滥用、故障排查、法律合规。我们不会将你的内容用于广告或出售给第三方。

## 七、存储与保护

- 因**无需注册**，我们不保存你的账号信息；
- 全部功能数据（截图、录屏、识别文本等）都保存在**你的设备本地**（你选择的目录或应用的标准目录）；
- 日志/诊断信息保留【如 30 天】后删除；
- 组件下载采用 HTTPS 加密，并采取合理的访问控制措施。

## 八、你的权利

由于无需注册，我们不持有你的账号数据。你可随时联系我们，查询或删除我们保存的与你有任何关联的数据。联系方式：taoge_ndt@163.com。

## 九、未成年人

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童提供服务。如你为未成年人，请在监护人指导下使用。
